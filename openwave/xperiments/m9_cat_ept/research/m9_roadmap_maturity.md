## M9.102 evidence effect

The evidence authority now records three distinct layers:

1. **implementation** — an equation, solver, adapter, or evolution exists and executes;
2. **state/physical sub-gate** — the stationary, packet-reduction, calibration, or full-Einstein gate passes;
3. **physical interpretation** — identity, independent calibration, and external prediction remain separate.

Magnetic moment, electric force, and magnetic force no longer receive `reduced_constructed` merely because the coupled action exists. Their state axis advances only if the symmetry-reduced stationary-state gate passes. Gravity advances only on the explicit weak-field state gate.
