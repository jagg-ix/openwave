# M9.124 findings: three aspects of time

The audit found a naming mismatch. `ThreeClockReversibilitySpectrum` classifies unitary parameter time, modular time, and entropic time. Page-Wootters is formalized separately as the relational-conditioning mechanism.

For the requested synthesis the physically useful trinity is:

1. Page-Wootters: relational order and clock-frame choice;
2. modular flow: reversible state-dependent thermal order;
3. entropic time: irreversible accumulated change.

The repository already contains pairwise bridges, including dissipative Page-Wootters conditional dynamics and modular/entropic complementarity. It does not yet contain one theorem identifying all three parameters.

M9.124 therefore registers the role taxonomy, executes three reduced controls, and adds a fail-closed unification gate. The result supports a three-aspect framework while rejecting a single-clock equivalence claim.
