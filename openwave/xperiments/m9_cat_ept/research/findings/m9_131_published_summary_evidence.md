# M9.131 — Published summary evidence

M9.131 advances the existing-data pipeline from fixtures to values explicitly reported in published tables, fit summaries, and abstract-level bounds.

1. Four canonical publication manifests are registered with deterministic metadata digests.
2. Moreva 2017 Table I contributes three measured Leggett–Garg `K3` values with uncertainties.
3. Gustavsson 2016 contributes published quasiparticle-decay fit parameters; the committed trajectory is an equation reconstruction, not raw observations.
4. Lu 2003 contributes the published lower bound of `10 microseconds or more` for inelastic scattering in nearly isolated quantum dots.
5. The summary evaluators reproduce the published claims while preserving the distinction between publication summaries and raw-data validation.

## Boundary

Raw publisher-file digests, raw observation rows, and a real raw leave-one-carrier-out result remain required for external promotion. No new experiment is required before attempting that reanalysis.
