# M9.127 Planckian discrimination

The existing eight rounded material ratios support the broad Planckian band. A stricter leave-one-paper-out comparison gives a weaker result:

- the fixed value `R = 1` has lower aggregate absolute log error than a training-fitted constant;
- the fixed value wins only one of the three paper folds;
- therefore the paper-level preference is mixed and does not identify an entropic-time mechanism.

A prospective test requires raw temperatures, transport times, uncertainties, extraction methods, regime definitions, frozen exclusions, commitment-before-reveal, and an independent replication source.
