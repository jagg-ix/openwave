# M9.109 findings

The algebraic mass, clock, and inference-width forms of `G` agree exactly. They do not make ordinary particle clocks universal: electron, muon, and proton anchors imply radically different couplings. Measured `G` selects the Planck mass/frequency as the inversion control. A physical CAT/EPT prediction remains blocked until that universal scale is independently grounded without using `G`.
