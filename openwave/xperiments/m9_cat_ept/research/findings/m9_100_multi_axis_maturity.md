## Interpretation

`validated_in_scope` keeps the existing narrow validation discipline: a literal dimensionless, topological, algebraic, or free-wave criterion can close while physical identity and broader phenomenology remain outside that scope.

`conditional_validated` records strong closure whose named physical interpretation still depends on theorem hypotheses, stable-state existence, identity, calibration, or a broader covariant reduction.

`reduced_model_validated` records an honestly closed reduced carrier without equating it to the full field theory.

`calibration_pending` distinguishes a stable dimensionless construction from a missing physical-unit prediction.

`candidate` is reserved for structural or variational ingredients without the required dynamical state or predictive closure.

The reclassification changes epistemic reporting only. It does not alter any theorem, simulation result, physical identity, parameter, or prediction.
