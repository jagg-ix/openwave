# M9.130 — Existing-data evaluators and cross-carrier generalization

M9.130 advances from package validation to executable reuse of published experiments.

1. Canonical normalization accepts relational-conditioning and binary-relaxation rows with units, uncertainties, stable IDs, and split labels.
2. Theorem-specific evaluators score Page–Wootters conditioning fidelity and the exact binary relaxation/KL law with frozen rates.
3. A leave-one-carrier-out protocol tests generalization across relational photon, quantum-dot, and superconducting-qubit carriers without per-carrier refitting.

## Boundary

The committed fixtures validate the machinery only. Real source rows, verified digests, and real held-out cross-carrier results remain required for external promotion.
