# M9.69 — stationary non-Gaussian branch

Construct a normalized stationary solution of the full spatial cubic--quintic equation without assuming a Gaussian profile. Require convergence from super-Gaussian, anisotropic, and shell seeds; localization; a small stationary residual; and consistency across 20³, 24³, and 28³ grids. Retain the boundary that the numerical coefficients are still conditional on the M9.63 selection assumptions.
