# M9.119 task gates — gauge-covariant strong and electroweak carriers

## M9.119a — local SU(3) color carrier

Pass only if one finite periodic system supplies:

- local SU(3) matter and link transformation laws;
- a covariant lattice matter operator;
- gauge-covariant matter and link evolution;
- plaquette and rectangular Wilson observables;
- preserved link unitarity and unit determinant;
- nonzero non-Abelian commutator;
- explicit rejection of QCD, confinement, and quark-identity promotion.

A nonzero Wilson action or a small-loop value is not an area law.

## M9.119b — local SU(2)xU(1) Higgs carrier

Pass only if one finite periodic system supplies:

- local SU(2) and U(1) links;
- the U(1)^3 Higgs-doublet action used by the formal authority;
- gauge-covariant Higgs and link evolution;
- separate SU(2) and U(1) Wilson actions;
- quartic Higgs potential and relaxation toward `mu^2/(2 lambda)`;
- a residual U(1) stabilizer check;
- explicit rejection of full electroweak, fermion, Weinberg-angle, and mass claims.

A bosonic Higgs carrier is not the complete electroweak theory.

## M9.119c — authority and platform integration

Pass only if:

- the live Physlib branch head and root are exact;
- all six formal source blobs are pinned;
- the previous M9.117 authority remains valid;
- both numerical campaigns pass their local gauge-covariance gates;
- schema-v22 registration records the constructed layers and retained boundaries;
- stable current aliases, root registry, M9 profile, roadmap, and platform contract all report M9.119;
- no physical claim is promoted.

## Failure interpretation

A failed covariance or group-constraint gate is a software/model failure. A passed finite-carrier gate is only implementation evidence. QCD confinement, complete chiral electroweak dynamics, calibrated masses, decays, and external validation require separate M9.120 campaigns.
